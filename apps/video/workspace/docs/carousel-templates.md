# Carousel templates

Any carousel can be offered as a template. New carousel then offers three ways
to start: Blank, A template, or Write it with AI. Starting from a template
makes a copy. Tyler asked for "Blank or a template?" in this window.

- **Marking one:** "Offer as template" in a carousel's menu in the Carousels
  tab of the carousel editor. Picked again, "Stop offering as template".
- **Where templates are offered:** only in New carousel, under "A template",
  which appears once you have one. Tyler, 20 Oct 2026: no Templates tab.
- **Templates stay in their carousel project**, so one can be opened, edited
  and unmarked like any carousel.
- **This project's first.** New carousel lists the open carousel project's
  templates under "In this project", then the rest under "Other projects"
  (Tyler, 1 Nov 2026; [carousel-project-carousels.md](carousel-project-carousels.md)).
- **Starting from one** copies its slides, shape and caption under the name
  you give, into the open carousel project, adding that project's caption
  footer, with new ids for every slide and layer. Editing the copy never
  changes the template, and the copy is not a template itself.
- **Duplicating a template** makes an ordinary carousel.
- **Only your own.** Templates are per person, like carousels.
- **Saved as** `is_template` on `video_carousels` (`drizzle/0137`).
