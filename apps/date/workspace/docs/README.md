# Date's docs

What Date does and why, one file per part. Tyler's decisions of 17 Oct 2026 are in `../tasks/tinder/01-ai-tinder.md`, of 18 Oct 2026 in `../tasks/features/01-chats-and-replies.md`, `../tasks/features/02-swiping-and-scoring.md` and `../tasks/features/03-your-profile.md`, and of 22 Oct 2026 in `../tasks/facebook/01-facebook-dating.md`. They outrank the code.

- [phone/phone-link.md](phone/phone-link.md): the Date phone app, pairing, the signed messages and the plain actions a phone runs.
- [phone/screen-window.md](phone/screen-window.md): the phone's live screen on the dashboard, and taps from it.
- [tinder/walks.md](tinder/walks.md): what each Tinder walk reads and does, the walk kit every app shares, and how to refresh Tinder's labels after an update.
- [facebook/facebook-dating.md](facebook/facebook-dating.md): Facebook Dating as a profile, Tyler's decisions of 22 Oct 2026, and what it does: Likes you first, comments on high-score likes, mutual friends, Second Look, the weekly match, Report, the break, and Compare apps.
- [facebook/walks.md](facebook/walks.md): what each Facebook Dating walk does, staying inside Dating, and how to refresh Facebook's labels once a real phone reads them.
- [tinder/stop-screens-and-limits.md](tinder/stop-screens-and-limits.md): the screens that stop a profile, the check after an app updates, a frozen app, the limits (and Facebook's slower pace), and "a message is never sent twice".
- [ai/how-the-ai-decides.md](ai/how-the-ai-decides.md): the score, the swipe rule, drafting, the date timing, the automate switch and the Start/Stop loop.
- [ai/swiping-and-scoring.md](ai/swiping-and-scoring.md): learning from overruled swipes, Sonnet's first look and Opus's second, People I know, Super Likes, the swiping schedule, Tinder's own distance and ages, passed photos deleted after 30 days, What's working and what a match costs.
- [ai/spend.md](ai/spend.md): what the AI costs and the monthly cap.
- [your-profile.md](your-profile.md): the AI's suggestions for your own profile with before and after, Apply and Undo on the phone, new prompt answers from About me only, and the first-photo test.
- [launch.md](launch.md): the live site at date.barelycompany.com, where it runs, every live value and the release record.
- [settings.md](settings.md): the Dating settings, in the window the dashboard's cog opens.
- [profiles.md](profiles.md): several dating profiles for one member, each one app's account with its own phone, settings, leads, spend and swipe loop; two profiles sharing one phone; the picker, New profile, Pause and Delete.
- [dating-dashboard.md](dating-dashboard.md): the one three-column dashboard and Tyler's rules for it: leads and their status, the summary with the stats, the phone and My profile in the right panel, the Dates calendar, the cog's settings window, and the sample data.
- [chats/chats-and-replies.md](chats/chats-and-replies.md): openers, the one follow-up, what they told you, chats that are over or scams, they unmatched, Show 3, tone, Tidy, the member's voice, warming or cooling, Ask Date, dead matches and the Openers card.
- [chats/dates-and-calendar.md](chats/dates-and-calendar.md): a yes to a date, the Dates tab, Before your date, the Dates tab's month and Google Calendar.
- [Shell integration](shell-integration.md) — each shell merge into Date: what arrived, what stayed Date's own.
