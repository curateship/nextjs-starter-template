# What the AI costs

- **Every call goes through the shell's usage meter** (`runAiCall`), tagged `dating-read-card`, `dating-draft-reply` (answers, first messages, follow-ups, Show 3, tone and Tidy), `dating-chat-notes` (what they told you and a yes to a date), `dating-date-brief`, `dating-ask-matches` or `dating-review-profile`, so it shows on the AI usage screen like any other.
- **A new message from them costs two calls**: the notes call, small, text only and at low effort, then the draft. Show 3 is one call that writes three.
- **There is no table of Date's own.** The cap reads the meter's rows for those tags. Each row names its profile in `metadata.accountId`, because the shell's table has no column for it, so each profile's spend, cap and costs are its own (`../profiles.md`).
- **The prices are Date's own for now.** Claude Opus 5.5 is $4 per million tokens in and $20 out, and Claude Sonnet 5.5, the first look at every card, $2 in and $10 out, both from the claude-api skill's table cached 6 Oct 2026. The shell's price list (`src/lib/ai/ai-models.ts`, a shell file) has neither model yet, so the AI usage screen shows these calls at $0 until the shell adds them.
- **A card costs one Sonnet call**, and a second, Opus, call when Sonnet's score is within 10 of the like line. Someone on the People I know list costs nothing.
- **The cap**, $20 a month by default, is in the Dating settings, one per profile. Once this month's spend reaches it, no call is made. Swiping stops, and the Swiping section of the dashboard's phone panel says "Spend cap reached" and shows the spend against the cap. A button that needs the AI says the same in its message.
- **The cap is a pause, not a failure.** A card or a chat the cap held back keeps no error, so the AI reads or drafts it as soon as the month turns over or the cap is raised.
- **A card should cost about 1 to 3 cents** on Opus, and about half that on Sonnet alone, with up to nine photos shrunk to 768 pixels and the AI at low effort. That is an estimate from the token prices, not a measurement: the first real call, on 18 Oct 2026, was refused by Anthropic because the account has no credit left. Measure it on the first real card and correct this line.
- **What a match costs**: the dashboard's AI spend section divides this month's spend by this month's matches and agreed dates (`swiping-and-scoring.md`).
