# Posts of your own on LinkedIn

The Posts tab holds the project's own posts, reposts and newsletter issues.
New post makes a draft; Post sends it through the same checks as a comment:
the warm-up, the pace, the pause, and the workday hours. Tyler, 22 Oct 2026:
any LinkedIn account past its warm-up may post.

## A post

Words up to 3,000 characters, with one of:

- a picture from the media library;
- a video from the media library, an MP4, MOV or WebM up to 500 MB, the most
  the app uploads through a proxy;
- a poll: a question of 140 characters, two to four answers of 30, open 1, 3,
  7 or 14 days. Its votes are read back with the daily check.

Write with AI fills the box in the account's voice. A set hour must fall in
the account's workday, weekdays 8:00 to 18:00, or Post refuses it.

## The link goes in the first comment

LinkedIn shows a post with a link to fewer people. A link typed into a
post's words is taken out when the draft is saved, the post goes up without
it, and the link is posted as the first comment under it from the same
account, in the same visit. If the post goes up but the comment does not,
Try again posts only the comment, never the post a second time. The first
comment is the one comment that may carry a link; Tyler's "never a link in a
comment" is about comments on other people's posts.

## Tags

A post can tag people and company pages. Type `@Name` in the words and add
the person's page address beside it. The browser types the name into
LinkedIn's own picker, picks the entry whose page matches, and reads the box
back before Post. The AI never adds a tag by itself. A throwaway account
never tags an important one.

## A repost with your thoughts

Repost on a post in To look at makes a repost draft: a line of your own on
top of someone's post. Write with AI writes that line. A throwaway account
never reposts an important account's post.

## Starting from something that did well

Start from lists the project's published Medium articles and its
best-received Reddit comments still up. The AI rewrites the chosen one for
LinkedIn rather than copying it.

**Post ideas** reads the questions people asked in the project's LinkedIn
posts and their comments, and suggests posts that answer them. Each idea
names the post it came from.

## A newsletter issue

An issue of the account's LinkedIn newsletter goes to everyone who
subscribed. It has a title, a body and a cover picture. The AI writes the
outline first, then the issue from the outline. Starting from a Medium
article, the AI rewrites it in new words and a new order, so the two never
read as copies. The browser types the body a paragraph at a time; an issue
can take up to 30 minutes.

## Groups

The keyword row's ⋮ menu holds the project's groups. Paste a group's address
to add it; a search account reads its name and rules, which are handed to
the AI before it writes a post for that group. Post checks a group post
against those rules with the AI and refuses one that breaks them; a group
with no rules saved checks nothing. Rules
can be pasted by hand for a group that shows them to members only. A group
that has not let the account in refuses Post with one sentence.

## As a company page

An important account that runs a company page can comment and post as the
page. The pages are read from the account's "Comment as" list each time Promo
looks at who is signed in. Tyler, 22 Oct 2026: important accounts only, since
a page is the business's own name, so a throwaway is never offered one.

This does not work yet. Important accounts run on phones only, and the
LinkedIn app on a phone can't speak as a page, so pages are offered only on a
browser account, which an important account never is. Tyler's call is owed:
allow pages on browser accounts, or teach the phone to switch to a page.

## After it goes up

The daily check reads its reactions, comments and reposts, and the comments
under it, each with an Answer. Edit changes a post's words; Delete takes it
down. A newsletter issue is edited on LinkedIn itself.

## On a phone

The LinkedIn app on a phone posts words with at most one picture. A poll, a
video, a repost, tags, a group, a company page, a newsletter and a post with
a link need the account on a browser profile (the app never says where a post
landed, so the link's first comment could never follow), and Post says so
before anything is sent. Edit and Delete are browser-only too, so they are
not offered on a phone account's posts and comments.
